## 1. General provisions

1.1. This Privacy and Personal Data Processing Policy (the “Policy”) sets out what personal data the Corporate Endowment Foundation “Science and Education Endowment Fund” collects through the website https://endowment-fund.kz (the “Website”), why it does so, how the data are stored and protected, and what rights personal data subjects have.

1.2. The Policy has been prepared in accordance with the Constitution of the Republic of Kazakhstan, Law of the Republic of Kazakhstan No. 94-V of 21 May 2013 “On Personal Data and Their Protection” (the “Law”) and other legislation of the Republic of Kazakhstan.

1.3. The owner and operator of personal data is:

- Corporate Endowment Foundation “Science and Education Endowment Fund” (the “Fund”);
- BIN (business identification number): 260240001314;
- address: 11 Saraishyk St, Astana, Republic of Kazakhstan;
- email for personal data enquiries: endowment@alageum.com.

1.4. The Policy applies to the Website only. The Fund is not responsible for third-party websites linked from the Website.

1.5. The Fund processes personal data in line with the principles of lawfulness, purpose limitation, data minimisation, accuracy and confidentiality.

1.6. Key terms have the meanings given to them in the Law. Personal data means information relating to a specific individual, or an individual who can be identified on the basis of that information (the personal data subject).

## 2. What data we collect and why

2.1. The Fund collects only the data that visitors enter themselves in the Website forms, and technical data about visits (section 4). By form:

| Website form | Data | Purpose of processing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| “Write to us”, form on the Contacts page | name, email, topic, message | replying to the enquiry |
| Contributor request (For contributors page) | name, phone, email, type of contributor, intended amount, best time to contact, subject, message | contacting the prospective contributor, preparing an endowment agreement or donation agreement |
| Grant application | full name, organisation, email, phone, selected programme, project summary | reviewing the application under the Fund’s charitable programmes, contacting the applicant |
| News subscription | email | sending the Fund’s news and press materials |
| CV (Careers page) | name, email, desired position, CV file and the information in it, cover message | recruitment |

2.2. The Fund does not ask visitors to provide identity document details, individual identification number (IIN), bank details, health information or any other data not needed for the purposes listed. Please do not include such information in messages or CVs.

2.3. If additional documents are needed to conclude an agreement or review a grant application, the Fund will request them separately and obtain separate consent for them.

2.4. The Website is not intended for persons under 18. An application made on behalf of a minor must be submitted, and consent given, by the minor’s legal representative.

2.5. The Fund does not take decisions that have legal consequences for a data subject solely on the basis of automated data processing.

## 3. Consent to processing

3.1. A visitor gives consent to the processing of personal data by ticking the box “I agree to the processing of my personal data” and submitting the form. A form cannot be submitted without consent.

3.2. Consent covers the actions needed for the purposes in section 2: collection, recording, systematisation, storage, updating, use, transfer in the cases set out in section 5, depersonalisation, blocking and destruction.

3.3. Consent remains valid until the purpose of processing is achieved or until it is withdrawn (section 8).

## 4. Cookies and web analytics

4.1. When you visit the Website, technical data are transmitted automatically: IP address, device and browser type, language, referral source, pages viewed, time and duration of the visit.

4.2. The Website uses:

- **essential browser data** — remembers the colour theme and accessibility settings the visitor has chosen; stored only in the visitor’s browser and not transmitted to the Fund;
- **Yandex Metrica** (Yandex LLC, Russian Federation) and **Google Analytics** (Google LLC, USA) — for anonymised visitor statistics and to improve the Website; these services set their own cookies;
- **Google Maps** on the Contacts page — to show the office location; the service may set its own cookies.

4.3. The Fund uses analytics data only in aggregate form and does not attempt to identify individual visitors from them.

4.4. Visitors can disable or delete cookies in their browser settings and opt out of analytics using the Yandex (https://yandex.ru/support/metrica/general/opt-out.html) and Google (https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout) tools. The Website will continue to work.

## 5. Transfer of data to third parties

5.1. The Fund does not sell personal data or share them for advertising.

5.2. The Fund uses the following services to operate the Website; each receives only the data needed for its function:

| Service | Function | Where data are processed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| FormSubmit (formsubmit.co) | delivers form submissions to the Fund’s email | USA |
| The Fund’s email service (alageum.com domain) | stores received requests and correspondence | Republic of Kazakhstan |
| Netlify, Inc. | Website hosting | USA |
| Yandex LLC, Google LLC | web analytics, maps | Russian Federation, USA |

5.3. By submitting a form, the visitor consents to the cross-border transfer of the data entered in it through the services listed above, within the purposes set out in section 2.

5.4. The Fund may also transfer personal data:

- to government authorities — on the grounds and in the manner established by the legislation of the Republic of Kazakhstan;
- to members of the Fund’s Board of Trustees and its experts — to review grant applications and contributors’ proposals, provided that they keep the data confidential;
- to other persons — only with the data subject’s separate consent.

5.5. The Fund does not publish the personal data of applicants and contributors without their consent. Information about grant recipients and contributors is disclosed only where required by the legislation on endowment funds or with the consent of the persons concerned.

## 6. Retention periods

6.1. Personal data are kept no longer than needed for the purposes of processing, unless a longer period is required by law:

| Data | Retention period |
| --- | --- |
| Enquiries via the “Write to us” and Contacts forms | 1 year after the reply |
| Contributor requests | 3 years after the last contact; if an agreement is concluded — for the retention period of the agreement documents |
| Grant applications | 5 years after the decision on the application |
| News subscription | until the visitor unsubscribes |
| CVs | 1 year after receipt, if the candidate is not hired |

6.2. When the period expires or consent is withdrawn, the data are destroyed or depersonalised, except where the law requires them to be kept.

## 7. Protection of personal data

7.1. The Fund takes legal, organisational and technical measures to protect personal data against unlawful access, alteration, blocking, copying, dissemination and destruction, including:

- transmission of data from the Website over the secure HTTPS protocol;
- access to requests limited to Fund employees who need it for their duties;
- employees’ undertakings not to disclose personal data;
- appointment of a person responsible for organising personal data processing.

7.2. In the event of a personal data breach, the Fund takes measures to remedy it and notifies data subjects and the authorised body in the cases and manner established by law.

## 8. Rights of the personal data subject

8.1. The personal data subject has the right to:

- know whether the Fund processes their personal data and receive information about them;
- request that their data be changed or supplemented where there are grounds to do so;
- request that data processed in breach of the law be blocked or destroyed;
- withdraw consent to processing, except where this would contradict the law or an unfulfilled obligation;
- unsubscribe from mailings via the link in the email or by writing to the Fund’s email;
- appeal against the Fund’s actions to the authorised body for personal data protection or to the court.

8.2. Withdrawal of consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before the withdrawal. After consent is withdrawn, the Fund may be unable to review an application or continue correspondence.

## 9. How to contact the Fund

9.1. Requests concerning personal data should be sent in writing to endowment@alageum.com with the subject line “Personal data”, or by post to: Science and Education Endowment Fund, 11 Saraishyk St, Astana.

9.2. Please state your name, contact details for the reply and the nature of your request. The Fund may ask you to confirm that the request comes from the data subject.

9.3. The Fund replies within the time limits established by the Law.
